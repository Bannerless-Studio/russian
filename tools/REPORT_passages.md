## Manual QA (2026-09-24, QA round 2 applied)

- **Source and build.** The 60 passages are hand-written for this pack in
  `tools/passages_src.json` (`"src": "gen"`). `packbuilder passages` writes
  `pack/passages.json` and this report. Two runs give byte-identical output.
- **Builder (vocab-engine 3fb879c + Russian port).** The link-context check
  and the declared `oop` keys compare ё-folded lemmas (build keys are еще,
  the pack says ещё; `oop` may say ёлка, солёный). Token spans are located in
  ё-folded text (`span_fold`). The one-shot `refill_unexampled` drop is now
  mirrored by the shared `finish_words`. Passage-only token rules in
  `langs/ru.py` (the corpus build never runs them): a token read as an
  adjective or numeral whose spelling is a pack adverb links the adverb
  (хорошо, лучше, тихо, странно, больше, not хороший/тихий); меньше links
  мало; стоит/стоят is стоять unless a price or an infinitive follows
  (стоит посмотреть, сколько стоит); "Тому, кто" is the correlative тот,
  not Tom; capitalised Новый год is новый + год; a sign-off "Целую" is
  целовать; "друг другу / друг с другом" links nothing. An ellipsis
  ("Так... Вижу") starts a new sentence, so the next capital is not a name.
- **Link audit.** Every span whose surface is spelled like a pack lemma but
  links another word was listed and read. The remaining ones are intended:
  existential есть -> быть, object pronouns его/её/их -> он/она/они,
  "все вместе" -> весь, "больше всего", "одно и то же", "Чем могу помочь",
  "в прошлое воскресенье", and noun дома (старые дома, из дома).
  Unlinked word tokens are names, quoted titles (Солнце, Дорога домой, Волна,
  Мой город...), declared out-of-pack words, the numerals двухсот/оба, and
  the reciprocal друг другу.
- **Names.** Capitalised names are skipped. Sentence-initial names or
  one-word options that the truecaser lowers (Бим, Нина, Оля, Лайма, Ольга,
  Ван...) are declared in `oop` with the reason "name". The p0018 student is
  Ван: the name Ли was read as the particle ли.
- **Vocabulary.** The pack is subtitle-ranked, so many everyday words are
  missing (кафе, торт, пешком, редко, школьник, паспорт). Texts avoid them.
  21 out-of-pack lemmas remain, each needed by its topic and listed with a
  reason (recipe steps, собеседование, спектакль, зонт, приют, ёлка...).
- **Tenses.** A1 is mostly present tense, but some A1 texts use the past
  (p0004, p0011, p0013, p0019) and the future (p0005, p0015). A2 uses past
  and future freely; B1 adds conditional and participles.
- **Question checks.** Every question's `words` are linked in its sentence
  and each question was read against that sentence. No tf item copies its
  sentence and no run of 5+ equal tf answers exists. No B1 correct option
  repeats 4+ consecutive words of its sentence. The correct mc option is the
  unique longest in 24 of 163 (15%); correct answers sit at positions
  0/1/2/3 as 48/40/36/39.
