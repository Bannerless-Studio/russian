## QA round 1 (2026-09-24)

- Word sample, seed 31, 60 words (20 per level): 58/60 correct primary sense. The misses were здравствовать, a verb lemma left from the greeting's tokens, and видно ("it is visible" instead of "apparently"). Both are fixed now: a drop_key and an override.
- Top 300 by rank: 0 wrong POS after the rules below.
- Sentence sample, seed 32, 90 sentences, 520 links: 3 wrong links (99.4%), 87/90 fully correct. The wrong links are: Тому -> то, a name read as the word; среда glossed "Wednesday" where it means "medium", now fixed by override; что conj in "что это за".
- Kelly cross-check: see the table above. It is used for sanity only and never shipped.
- Rules added in this round, each for a class seen in the scans or samples:
  - ё/е folding on every side; spelling and stress restored from kaikki.
  - Letter names and abbreviation pointers are not words (п -> параграф, мм -> миллиметр); simplemma abbreviation expansions are rejected.
  - Finite "есть" -> быть. Object его/её/их -> он/она/они.
  - Plural все/всех/всем -> весь. The сам forms are one pronoun. бы is a particle, not AUX.
  - Pure prepositions tagged ADV are read as prepositions.
  - Predicatives (надо, можно) come from the Wiktionary "adj [predicative]" entries.
  - Deverbal nouns tagged noun-from-verb (родитель, стоимость) are lemmas.
  - "archaic" + "standard" tags describe an alternative form (судья), not the senses.
  - Hyphenated words stay one token.
  - The capitalisation-based proper-noun test is off in the pool (Земля, Бог, Советский), but capitalised mid-sentence tokens are still never linked.
  - The rare-reading threshold is lowered (смог -> смочь, not "smog").
  - Aspect partners keep one gloss.
  - 84 gloss overrides.
## QA round 2 (2026-09-24)

- Word sample, seed 31, 60 words: 60/60 correct primary sense. Every A1 and A2 content gloss was hand-skimmed; 163 A1/A2 glosses are now hand-set in gloss_overrides.json (95 overrides added this round, 178 in total).
- Sentence sample, seed 32, 90 sentences, 523 links: 4 wrong links (99.2%), 86/90 fully correct. The wrong links are что conj in "Мало ли что" and "что ты хочешь", узнаем -> узнавать (ё/е fold), and "с тех пор" -> пора. Residuals are in TODO.md.
- Rules added in this round, each for a class:
  - Gloss style leaks are cut by a shared strip list (core/gloss.strip_gloss_style): "mutually reflexive", "diminutive:", quoted words, thy/hither/whither, "to dun", "one wants", "as ... as".
  - Irregular surface -> lemma table (цветы -> цветок). Capitalised name homographs (Том, Тома, Тому, Миссис) are names and never link.
  - всё vs весь is routed by the corpus spelling and the next token: singular всё before a verb or a short-form predicate is всё "everything"; before a noun/adjective it is весь.
  - An interjection homograph of a preposition or conjunction ("О нет!") links to nothing.
  - Hyphenated words that are Wiktionary headwords get their wordfreq rank from the joined form (кто-то, что-то, из-за).
  - Forced A1: ordinals 1-5, миллион, свидание, извинить, уметь, дедушка, бабушка, простите; ничего is a pronoun.
  - Words with no example sentence are dropped and replaced by the next word by rank; мм/см are abbreviations and not words.
  - A1 example sentences are penalised for participles/gerunds, который, and rare content words.
  - Sensitive sentences are kept out of A1/A2: sexual content, suicide, threats/violence, dying/death wishes, weapons (shared English list + Russian terms). Rape and sexual/child abuse (`drop_all_levels`) are removed at every level; check fails on a match.
  - Gloss-level scan (shared vulgar/sexual English list): a matching sense never leads a gloss; check fails on any A1/A2 match.
- Word ids are frozen in tools/id_map_v1.json (key: folded lemma|pos).
- 2026-09-25: engine bumped to 059b90b (voice-init fix, no-voice notice timing, word ceiling). 8 words moved A2->B1 under the shared word-level ceiling: убить, убийство, кровь, оружие, погибнуть, убивать, секс, убийца. Band-edge shifts from re-counting: народ, область, держать A2->A1; сад, потеря, появляться, единый, выпустить, вера, использование, ответственность B1->A2. No rank, id or gloss changed.
