# tools/ — builder inputs (dev/agent notes)

Purpose: everything the Russian pack build reads or writes, other than the
shared builder itself (`engine/tools/packbuilder`, `langs/ru.py`). Read this
before touching any file here; it says what is hand-maintained vs generated.

## Files

- `build_pack.py` — shim. Runs `python3 -m packbuilder build --lang ru --repo .`
  against `engine/tools` (or `$PACKBUILDER_PATH`). Hand-maintained, rarely changes.
- `requirements.txt` — hand-maintained. Shared builder deps plus pymorphy3
  and the Russian spaCy model (`ru_core_news_sm`).
- `gloss_overrides.json` — hand-maintained. Hand gloss fixes for
  high-frequency words; all A1/A2 glosses were hand-skimmed in QA round 2.
- `forced_a1.txt` — hand-maintained. A1 core word list forced into A1 (closed
  sets live in `langs/ru.py` instead).
- `passages_src.json` — hand-maintained. Source text for the 60 reading
  passages; `packbuilder passages --lang ru` builds `pack/passages.json` from it.
- `id_map_v1.json` — generated, then frozen. Maps (lemma, POS) to a fixed
  word id across rebuilds. Never hand-edit; never renumber.
- `REPORT.md` — generated on every full build by `build_pack.py`. Everything
  outside the `manual` markers is regenerated; the manual section (QA
  verdicts, check output) is preserved across runs.
- `REPORT_passages.md` — generated by `packbuilder passages`. Per-passage
  coverage numbers and QA notes.

## Rebuild

See CLAUDE.md's "Commands" section for the pinned invocations. Sources
download once into `.cache/` (gitignored) and are reused; the build is
deterministic, so a cached re-run reproduces byte-identical `pack/*.json`.
The spaCy tagging pass over the ~710k English-translated Tatoeba sentences
takes about 9-10 minutes the first time and is cached; later runs take 2-4
minutes. `build_pack.py --stage {corpus,tag,lex,freq,words,all}` stops after
one stage.

## How words, senses and links are chosen

The shared pipeline is in `engine/tools/packbuilder/README.md`. The Russian
module (`langs/ru.py`) adds:

1. **ё/е.** Subtitles, Tatoeba and the tagger's training data write ё
   inconsistently. Every matching side is folded ё→е and stress marks are
   stripped; the displayed word restores Wiktionary's spelling (ещё,
   зелёный) and carries the е spelling as an `alt`, so cloze matches either
   spelling. Typing never requires ё/е or stress.
2. **Lemmas.** Tokens tagged with spaCy `ru_core_news_sm` (lemmas from
   pymorphy3), validated against Wiktionary. Verbs are infinitives. Aspect
   pairs (делать/сделать), motion-verb pairs (идти/ходить, ехать/ездить)
   and -ся verbs are separate entries; every verb gloss ends in "(impf.)",
   "(pf.)" or "(impf./pf.)". люди is its own lemma (pymorphy gives человек).
   Finite "есть" (is) links to быть; only the infinitive is "to eat".
   Object его/её/их link to он/она/они; possessive его/её/их are their own
   entries. "кто-то", "из-за", "по-русски" stay one token.
3. **Nouns** are shown bare; the gloss ends in the gender (" (m)", " (f)",
   " (n)", " (m/f)", " (pl.)" for plural-only nouns).
4. **Filters.** Letter names and abbreviation pointers are not words. Proper
   nouns and capitalised mid-sentence tokens are never linked. Sentences
   with profanity, non-standard English register, or more than 14 tokens
   are skipped.
5. **Sentences.** A1 example sentences prefer nominative/accusative nouns,
   with the prepositional allowed after в/на; genitive, dative and
   instrumental come from A2.
6. **Closed sets** (days, months, seasons, numbers 0-20, tens, сто, тысяча,
   colours, core greetings/conjunctions/prepositions) plus `forced_a1.txt`
   are forced into A1; everything else is levelled by frequency rank: A1
   600, A2 700, B1 700.

## Reading passages

`packbuilder passages --lang ru .` builds `pack/passages.json` from
`passages_src.json` and writes `REPORT_passages.md`. It links word ids the
same way as the Tatoeba sentences, and enforces in-pack coverage of at least
95% at A1/A2 and 93% at B1, plus a level budget (an A1 passage may use at
most 3 A2 words, an A2 passage at most 3 B1 words). Russian-specific passage
linker rules (adjective/numeral surface reads as a pack adverb, меньше→мало,
стоит/стоят disambiguation, "Тому, кто", "Новый год", "Целую" sign-off,
"друг другу" no-link, ellipsis sentence breaks) live in `langs/ru.py`'s
passage hooks, not in this repo — see `../TODO.md`. Regenerate `pack/*.js`
afterward (jsonify_pack.py appends passages into sentences.js).

for full counts.
